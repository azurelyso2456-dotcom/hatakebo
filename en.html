Record what you planted and when. Add compost or fertiliser notes, then look back at previous years when deciding what to grow next.

For a small garden or an individual allotment plot. New plots can be 0.5–15 m on each side. Measurements use metres and centimetres; planting dates and crop choices are yours to decide.

Before you head outside

Your usual notebook, even without a signal.

Open Hatakebo with an internet connection and wait for “Ready to use offline”. Then you can reopen it, view your garden and save entries without a connection, on the same device and browser.

Your garden records stay in your browser, not on a Hatakebo server. Devices do not sync automatically. Download a backup from the menu and keep a separate copy in case of device loss or browser data deletion.

A few things to know

Questions before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. Garden layouts, crop and task records, backups and printing are free. No account is needed.

Does it give local planting advice?

No. A short selection of familiar garden crops comes first, followed by the rest in alphabetical order. This is not planting advice. Check local growing advice and seed packets. The language setting does not select a country or climate.

Does it prevent crop rotation problems?

It flags repeated crop groups using the planting history you have entered. It does not diagnose soil, predict disease or guarantee prevention. Use it as a prompt to review your records.

Can I move my records to another device?

Download a backup on the old device and import it on the new one. Importing replaces records rather than merging them. All five language versions share records when used on this site in the same browser.

Where can I get help?

Open Help & FAQ in the app menu for guidance on beds, planting, tasks, printing, backups and offline use. Contact opens an English Google Form. An internet connection is needed to open and submit it.
