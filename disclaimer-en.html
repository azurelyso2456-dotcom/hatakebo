About your data and disclaimer

English information updated: 10 October 2026

Your garden records stay with you

Hatakebo stores your garden layouts and records in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a Hatakebo server. Japanese, English and Indonesian pages on this same site use the same records. There is no automatic account backup or synchronisation between devices.

This is separate from website traffic statistics. Online visits fetch public files from the hosting provider and may contact Google as described below. External contact forms have their own data handling.

Website statistics: Google Analytics 4

The Japanese app, the English and Indonesian apps, and the English and Indonesian introduction pages use Google Analytics 4 (GA4), provided by Google, to understand visits and improve Hatakebo. This data notice and the step-by-step guides do not include a GA4 tag.

When the tag runs, it connects to Google and may use first-party cookies such as _ga to distinguish browsers and sessions. Statistics can include page addresses and titles, referring pages, visits and engagement, browser language, device and browser information, and approximate geographic location. Depending on the settings of the Google Analytics data stream, enhanced measurement may also record events such as scrolling, outbound link clicks or form interactions. This is not a claim that all access data is completely anonymous.

Our integration does not read your garden records from browser storage or add garden names, bed layouts, planting records, notes or backup contents to the analytics events it configures. We do not configure a personal User-ID or custom garden-record events. Website statistics are separate from the local notebook and are not a backup of it.

The tag loads after the page finishes loading. It is skipped if the browser reports that it is offline, has data-saving mode enabled, or has a slow connection (slow-2g/2g or an estimated downlink of 1 Mbps or less). These connection signals are not available in every browser. GA4 creates additional network traffic outside the app’s offline files; offline work is not deliberately queued by Hatakebo for later analytics upload. Blocking analytics does not prevent you from using the notebook.

This integration does not currently provide an on-site analytics consent or opt-out control. Where supported, you can use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on or your browser’s tracking controls. Do not clear all site data just to stop analytics: doing so can also delete your garden records. Keep a backup first.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Prepare for offline use

Open the app online and wait for “Ready to use offline”. Afterwards you can reopen it and keep records in the same browser without a connection. First-time preparation, updates and external websites require a connection. Browser storage may be cleared or evicted, which can remove offline files or records.

Keep backups

Records can be lost if site data is cleared, a device is reset or replaced, private browsing data is removed, or browser settings and storage change. Use Menu → Backup regularly and keep a copy elsewhere. Hatakebo cannot recover records from a server.

Importing a backup replaces the records on the destination device; it does not merge them. Back up those records first if you need them.

Growing guidance has limits

Planting-month information is based on general conditions in Japan. English is a language option, not a UK growing calendar. Climate, soil, variety and growing conditions affect results. Consult local gardening or agricultural advisers for specialist decisions.

Crop rotation reminders use the crops you recorded. They do not diagnose soil or disease, predict problems or guarantee prevention. Information is not guaranteed to be accurate or complete.
