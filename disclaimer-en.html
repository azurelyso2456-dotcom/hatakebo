About your data and disclaimer

English information updated: 12 October 2026

Your garden records stay with you

Your garden layout, crops and notes are saved in the browser on your device. Hatakebo does not automatically upload these records to its servers.

You can switch languages in the same browser and keep using the same records. Use Menu → Backup from time to time to keep a separate copy.

Understanding how Hatakebo is used

The English version does not use Google Analytics. Only the Japanese app uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to help improve Hatakebo.

Your garden records are not included in analytics data.

More about analytics and how to turn it off

If you open the Japanese app, GA4 uses cookies and sends information to Google about page visits, device and browser details, and approximate location. This applies wherever you access the Japanese app. Depending on its settings, analytics may also measure scrolling, external-link clicks and form interactions.

The other language versions and all introduction pages, guides and data notices do not use Google Analytics. The integration does not read garden records from browser storage or set a personal User-ID.

On supported browsers, you can use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or tracking-prevention settings. The notebook still works when analytics is blocked. There is currently no in-app analytics consent or off switch. To keep your garden records, choose a method that does not clear site data.

Analytics starts after the Japanese app loads. It is skipped when the browser reports an offline, data-saving or slow connection; not every browser provides this information. Its traffic is separate from offline preparation. Hatakebo does not queue your offline gardening activity for later analytics uploads.

Online visits download public files from the hosting provider. Information you submit through an external contact form is handled under that service’s policies. There is no automatic server backup or device synchronisation of your garden records.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Prepare for offline use

Open the app online and wait for “Ready to use offline”. Afterwards you can reopen it and keep records in the same browser without a connection. First-time preparation, updates and external websites require a connection. Browser storage may be cleared or evicted, which can remove offline files or records.

Keep backups

Records can be lost if site data is cleared, a device is reset or replaced, private browsing data is removed, or browser settings and storage change. Use Menu → Backup regularly and keep a copy elsewhere. Hatakebo cannot recover records from a server.

Importing a backup replaces the records on the destination device; it does not merge them. Back up those records first if you need them.

Growing guidance has limits

Planting-month information is based on general conditions in Japan. English is a language option, not a UK growing calendar. Climate, soil, variety and growing conditions affect results. Consult local gardening or agricultural advisers for specialist decisions.

Crop rotation reminders use the crops you recorded. They do not diagnose soil or disease, predict problems or guarantee prevention. Information is not guaranteed to be accurate or complete.
